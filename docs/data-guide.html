Public data guide

Where Nightcore Tracker's public data lives and what it means: the manifest, dated ranking snapshots, discovery editions, channels and the RSS feed, with field meanings, units, null handling, time zone, cutoff, coverage and completeness status.

Everything below is plain JSON or XML served next to the pages, generated once a day from public YouTube counters. Only these minimized public fields are published; the tracker's database, credentials and private endpoints are never exposed. Relative paths resolve against the site URL https://nightcore-tracker.com/, which the manifest also names.

Conventions

  • Time zone. Every date (YYYY-MM-DD) is a Europe/Madrid calendar day. Timestamps are UTC in the form YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ.
  • Cutoff. data_as_of is the newest observation behind a ranking date: the data cutoff. Rankings do not reflect anything counted after it.
  • Units. views, views_start and views_gained are counts of YouTube views from public counters; subscribers is a subscriber count; growth_pct is a percentage (100 * views_gained / views_start) rounded half away from zero to one decimal. Ordering always uses the exact ratio.
  • Missing values. null means unknown or unavailable, never zero. A ranking that is null (or absent from an older schema) is unavailable, which is different from an empty list (calculated, no eligible entries).
  • Coverage. Rankings cover monitored channels only, not all of YouTube. Shorts are excluded from song charts; mixes are not classified separately.
  • Completeness. collection_run.verified is true only when the day's collection runs completed. An unverified date (for example a forced publication after an incomplete video collection) may be missing observations; its collection_run.id is null and the pages show a warning.
  • Methodology. How the rankings work: seven-day windows, thresholds, tie-breaking and movement.
  • Freshness and retention. The site is rebuilt daily after collection. Dated snapshots are kept for the latest 5 dates listed in the manifest; edition records are kept permanently. A published snapshot or edition never changes.

data/channels.json (schema_version 1)

snapshot_date (the manifest's latest date), timezone, collection_run, selection (metric, limit, eligible_count) and channels (channel_id, name, url, avatar_url or null, views, subscribers or null), ordered by views and then channel_id. It always describes the latest date only.
