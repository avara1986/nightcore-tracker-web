Methodology

Rankings cover monitored channels only. For each ranking date (Europe/Madrid), the seven-day gain is the view counter at the cutoff minus the counter seven days earlier, and growth is that gain as a percentage of the starting counter. Both observations must exist and be roughly a week apart (within 6.0 hours); otherwise the entry is left out.

Missing observations are never treated as zero, a zero baseline has no percentage, new uploads wait until they have a full week of history, and counter corrections that go down are never ranked. Ties are broken by gain and then by YouTube ID. Movement compares against the complete ranking of the previous day, not only its top 10.

Breakout songs and Rising channels rank relative growth, so a smaller song or channel that grows faster can rank above a bigger one that gained more views. Top songs and Top channels rank the absolute number of views gained, which favours large, established entries. A song or channel can appear in both kinds of chart.

Rising channels only considers channels whose total view counter was already at a minimum baseline when the week started100,000 total views); views gained during the week do not count towards it. This keeps tiny starting counters from producing inflated percentages. Its movement compares against the previous day's complete percentage ranking, with that day's own baseline.

Channel figures use the channel's public YouTube total view counter, which also counts Shorts and uploads that are not monitored, so they need not match the songs listed above. Shorts are excluded from the song charts; mixes are not classified separately.
