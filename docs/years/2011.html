Top new channels: monitored channels created on YouTube in 2011 (Europe/Madrid calendar), ranked by their latest public total channel view counter; each lists its most viewed songs of all time. Channels without a creation date, without any channel counter or without any counted song are not ranked.

Top channels by songs published: a channel's views for 2011 are the sum of the latest public view counters of the songs it published in 2011 (Europe/Madrid calendar); each lists its most viewed songs of the year. Songs without a publication date are not counted.

Both rankings cover monitored channels only. Shorts are excluded and songs without any counter are not counted. Ties are broken by YouTube ID.

Shorts count as uploads, and channels with no scanned uploads or an undated one stay active. Active channels are listed first in both rankings, because they are the ones keeping nightcore alive right now; inactive channels follow, in the same order.
