Top channels by songs published: a channel's views for 2023 are the sum of the latest public view counters of the songs it published in 2023 (Europe/Madrid calendar); each lists its most viewed songs of the year. Songs without a publication date are not counted.

Shorts count as uploads, and channels with no scanned uploads or an undated one stay active. Active channels are listed first in both rankings, because they are the ones keeping nightcore alive right now; inactive channels follow, in the same order.
