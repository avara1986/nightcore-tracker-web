Top channels by songs published: a channel's views for 2017 are the sum of the latest public view counters of the songs it published in 2017 (Europe/Madrid calendar); each lists its most viewed songs of the year. Songs without a publication date are not counted.

Active channels are listed first in both rankings, because they are the ones keeping nightcore alive right now; inactive channels follow, in the same order.
